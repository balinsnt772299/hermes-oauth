Hermes Agent

A private, single-user automation agent.

What this is. Hermes Agent is a personal automation assistant that runs on its owner's own private server. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it is not distributed or offered for sale. It connects to the owner's own Google Account so that the owner can ask it to work with his own mail, files, calendar and documents.

Who uses it

Exactly one person — the owner of the Google Account it is connected to. There is no sign-up, no multi-tenancy, and no way for anyone else to authorise it. Access is granted through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent flow and can be revoked by the owner at any time.

What it does

  • Reads and searches the owner's own Gmail messages and labels
  • Sends mail on the owner's behalf, only when explicitly instructed
  • Reads, creates and downloads files in the owner's own Google Drive
  • Reads and creates events in the owner's own Google Calendar
  • Reads the owner's own Contacts, Google Sheets and Google Docs
  • Produces summaries, briefings and personal notes from that data

How your data is handled

All processing happens on the owner's private server. Data is not sent to third-party services and is not used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail, and the Terms of Service for the terms of use.
